## 3. ROS 1 vs ROS 2 — decided, with evidence

Upstream PragmaBot is **ROS 1** (catkin, rospy). We **ported to ROS 2**
(`ament_python`, rclpy) rather than bridging.

Armin prototyped the alternative — keep the planner on ROS 1, run an HTTP
server in the ROS 2 container wrapping MoveIt (`pragmabot/armin/`, **untracked**,
in `.gitignore:74`). That approach is sound; his server is properly built
(ReentrantCallbackGroup, MultiThreadedExecutor, timeouts, cancel).

We ported because **only `scene_observer.py` touches ROS** — one file, and the
version split disappears rather than being worked around. No HTTP hop, no JSON
schema on two sides, one ROS install. A ROS 2 action also gives feedback and
cancellation for free (his `/stop` endpoint exists to cover that gap).

**Two ideas worth stealing from `armin/`:**
- `/go_home` — joint-space move to a fixed config. That is the B3 observation
  retract. **Use OUR measured pose** (`CLAUDE.md:126`), not his SRDF `ready`:
  `[-0.181, -0.420, 0.005, -2.260, -0.008, 1.806, 0.609]`
- His `grasp_pipeline.py` uses subprocesses (nothing to start up) — the
  **fallback** if the ZMQ servers prove fragile in the lab.

**Naming trap:** Armin's "bridge" = ROS1↔ROS2. Our `bridge_node.py` =
planner↔robot, both sides ROS 2. Unrelated jobs, same word.

### `_scene_source` is deliberately None
`bridge_node` needs `(rgb, depth, intrinsics)` but does **not** subscribe to the
camera. Adding a subscription would put a second subscriber on the same
BEST_EFFORT topics the planner's `SceneObserver` already reads. It must be
injected. Until then every pick fails with a reason — by design, not a bug.

inv(T_camera_link<-optical)`. Result lands close to the pre-update
calibration (good physical sanity check). Old file backed up at
`~/.ros2/easy_handeye2/calibrations/fr3_zed_right.calib.bak-2026-08-24`.
**Caveat:** the live-TF offset was read from `tf2_echo`'s 3-decimal-place
printed output, not full precision (det ~0.9995 instead of 1.0) - fine for
fixing the gross misalignment, worth tightening from the ZED URDF/config
directly if higher precision is ever needed.
